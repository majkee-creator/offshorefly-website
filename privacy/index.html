Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Offshore Fly processes information when you use the Offshore Fly application and website.

This policy reflects the current feature set: Sign in with Apple, Apple in-app purchases, location-based mission planning, local app settings and third-party weather services.

1. Information processed

Location

With your permission, Offshore Fly uses device location to identify an operational area, centre maps and request relevant forecast data. Location permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. It is not used for advertising.

Sign in with Apple

Apple may provide a unique account identifier and, depending on your selection, your name or private relay email address. Apple controls the sign-in process.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Offshore Fly receives entitlement and transaction-status information required to unlock Professional Access, but does not receive your payment-card details.

Mission settings

Selected locations, limits, favourites, notes and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Support

When you contact support, we process the information you provide, such as your email address, message and diagnostic details.

2. Weather services

To obtain forecasts, Offshore Fly may send coordinates, requested time ranges and technical request information to atmospheric or marine data providers. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

3. Uses

  • Provide forecasts and mission-planning features.
  • Authenticate users and restore access.
  • Validate subscription entitlements.
  • Store app preferences.
  • Respond to support requests and maintain security.

4. Sharing

Offshore Fly does not sell personal information. Information is shared only where required for core functionality, including Apple authentication and purchases, weather providers, hosting and email services.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until removed or the app is deleted. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies. Support communications are kept only as reasonably necessary.

Deletion requests for information held directly by Offshore Fly can be sent to support@offshorefly.app.

6. Your choices

  • Change location access in iOS Settings.
  • Use manually selected locations where available.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data.

7. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, although no system guarantees absolute security.

8. Children

Offshore Fly is a professional operational tool and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as features or legal requirements change.
